INCUBATORS AND ACCELERATORS IN INDIA – KNOWLEDGE BASE
========================================================

## What is an Incubator?
An incubator provides long-term support (typically 1–5 years) to early-stage startups including office space, mentorship, networking, and sometimes seed funding. They are ideal for idea-stage or pre-revenue startups.

## What is an Accelerator?
An accelerator is a fixed-term (typically 3–6 months), cohort-based program that provides seed investment, mentorship, and connections in exchange for a small equity stake (typically 2–10%). Designed for startups that already have a prototype or early traction.

## Top Accelerators in India

### Y Combinator (Global, accepts India)
- Provides USD 500,000 in funding (USD 125,000 for 7% + USD 375,000 on MFN SAFE).
- 3-month program culminating in Demo Day.
- Notable India alumni: Meesho, Razorpay, ClearTax, Unacademy.
- Application: twice a year (S and W batches).

### T-Hub (Hyderabad, Telangana)
- India's largest startup incubator by space.
- Backed by Government of Telangana, IIIT-H, ISB, NALSAR.
- Programs: BootCamp, Scale, NEST (social entrepreneurs), Global Acceleration.
- Hosts 1,500+ startups.

### iCreate (Ahmedabad, Gujarat)
- International Centre for Entrepreneurship and Technology.
- Supported by Government of Gujarat.
- Sector-agnostic; strong in deep tech, clean energy.
- Provides prototyping labs, international exposure.

### NSRCEL (IIM Bangalore)
- N.S. Raghavan Centre for Entrepreneurial Learning.
- Backed by IIM Bangalore.
- Programs: Goldman Sachs 10KW (women entrepreneurs), NSRCEL Incubation.
- Strong mentorship and alumni network.

### IIT Madras Research Park (IITMRP)
- Over 250+ companies.
- Strong focus on deep tech, hardware, manufacturing.
- Access to IIT Madras faculty and research labs.
- NITI Aayog Atal Incubation Centre (AIC).

### CIIE.CO (IIM Ahmedabad)
- Centre for Innovation Incubation and Entrepreneurship.
- India's premier incubator for sustainable and social ventures.
- Programs: Bharat Inclusion Initiative (fintech), Enactus, CIIE Incubation.
- Strong focus on climate tech, fintech, agritech.

### Startup Village (Kerala)
- Asia's first telecom incubator.
- Backed by Kerala Startup Mission (KSUM).
- Strong hardware and IoT focus.
- Provides 24x7 co-working, prototyping labs, seed funding up to INR 10 lakh.

### Nasscom 10,000 Startups
- One of India's largest startup programs.
- 10,000 startups supported across 8 hubs.
- Provides co-working, mentorship, investor connections, product launch support.
- Strong SaaS and enterprise tech focus.

### Microsoft for Startups
- 3-month program with Azure credits (up to USD 120,000), technical support, market access.
- Available pan-India, no equity taken.
- Strong cloud, AI, enterprise software focus.

### Google for Startups Accelerator India
- 3-month equity-free acceleration program.
- Provides USD 200,000 in Google Cloud credits, mentorship from Google engineers.
- Focus on AI/ML, sustainability, women-led startups.
- Applications twice a year.

### AWS Activate
- Provides up to USD 100,000 in AWS credits.
- Available to early-stage startups.
- Access to AWS experts, technical training, partner network.

## Government-Backed Incubators (Atal Innovation Mission)
- Atal Incubation Centres (AIC): NITI Aayog funds 25+ AICs across India.
- Each AIC receives up to INR 10 crore over 5 years.
- Located in premier institutions (IITs, NITs, IIMs, central universities).

## Sector-Specific Incubators

### AgriTech
- MANAGE (Hyderabad): National Academy of Agricultural Research Management.
- AIC-MANAGE: INR 10 crore AIM-funded incubator for agri startups.
- IARI (New Delhi): Indian Agricultural Research Institute.

### HealthTech / MedTech
- Centre for Cellular and Molecular Platforms (C-CAMP), Bengaluru.
- AMTZ (Andhra Pradesh MedTech Zone), Visakhapatnam.
- BIRAC (Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council) – Bioincubation programme.

### EdTech
- SocialAlpha – focuses on social impact EdTech.
- FICCI FLO Incubation – women-led EdTech startups.

### FinTech
- IFINA (International Financial Services Centres Authority) – GIFT City, Gujarat.
- iSPIRT – industry think-tank for FinTech policy and community.

### CleanTech / GreenTech
- GERMI (Gujarat Energy Research and Management Institute).
- TERI (The Energy and Resources Institute) – incubation for clean energy startups.
- Villgro Innovations Foundation – rural social enterprise focus.
