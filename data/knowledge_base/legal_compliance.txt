LEGAL AND COMPLIANCE FOR STARTUPS IN INDIA – KNOWLEDGE BASE
=============================================================

## Business Structure Options

### Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd)
- Most preferred structure for funded startups.
- Governed by the Companies Act, 2013.
- Requires minimum 2 directors and 2 shareholders; max 200 shareholders.
- Registered with Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) via SPICe+ form.
- Mandatory annual filings: MCA Annual Return (MGT-7), Financial Statements (AOC-4), Auditor Appointment (ADT-1).
- Mandatory audits required regardless of turnover.
- Foreign investment (FDI) is permitted under automatic route for most sectors.
- Suitable for: VC-funded startups, products requiring IP protection.

### Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)
- Governed by the LLP Act, 2008.
- No restriction on maximum partners.
- Less compliance burden than Pvt Ltd.
- Foreign investment is possible but requires RBI approval (not automatic route).
- Annual filings: Form 8 (Statement of Account & Solvency), Form 11 (Annual Return).
- Suitable for: Professional services, consulting, bootstrapped startups.

### One Person Company (OPC)
- Governed by Companies Act, 2013.
- Single founder can incorporate.
- Must convert to Pvt Ltd when paid-up capital exceeds INR 50 lakh or turnover exceeds INR 2 crore.
- Suitable for: Solo founders, freelancers scaling up.

### Sole Proprietorship / Partnership
- No separate legal entity.
- Unlimited personal liability.
- Not suitable for funded or high-growth startups.

## Step-by-Step Company Incorporation (Pvt Ltd)

1. Obtain Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) for all proposed directors.
2. Apply for Director Identification Number (DIN) through MCA portal.
3. Reserve company name via RUN (Reserve Unique Name) service on MCA.
4. File SPICe+ (Simplified Proforma for Incorporating Company Electronically) form:
   - Includes PAN, TAN, EPFO, ESIC, Bank Account opening, GST registration (optional).
5. Prepare and file Memorandum of Association (MoA) and Articles of Association (AoA).
6. Receive Certificate of Incorporation (CoI) from Registrar of Companies (RoC).
7. Apply for PAN and TAN of the company.
8. Open a company bank account with the CoI and PAN.

## Key Registrations Required

### GST (Goods and Services Tax)
- Mandatory if annual turnover exceeds INR 40 lakh (goods) or INR 20 lakh (services); INR 10 lakh for special category states.
- E-commerce operators must register regardless of turnover.
- Filing: GSTR-1 (monthly/quarterly), GSTR-3B (monthly), GSTR-9 (annual).
- GST Rate varies: 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28% depending on product/service.

### PAN (Permanent Account Number)
- Mandatory for all companies; apply through NSDL/UTIITSL.

### TAN (Tax Deduction Account Number)
- Mandatory for deducting TDS on salaries, vendor payments, rent.

### MSME / Udyam Registration
- Optional but provides access to government schemes, priority lending, and protection from delayed payments.
- Register at udyamregistration.gov.in.

### Shop and Establishment Registration
- Required by most states for any commercial premises.
- Apply with the local municipal authority within 30 days of starting business.

### Professional Tax Registration
- Applicable in states like Maharashtra, Karnataka, West Bengal, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu.
- Employer deducts professional tax from employee salaries.

### ESIC (Employee State Insurance Corporation)
- Mandatory if employing 10+ employees.
- Employer contribution: 3.25%, Employee contribution: 0.75% of wages.

### EPF (Employee Provident Fund)
- Mandatory if employing 20+ employees.
- Employer contribution: 12%, Employee contribution: 12% of basic salary.

## Intellectual Property (IP) Protection

### Trademark
- Register your brand name/logo with the Trademark Registry (ipindia.gov.in).
- Time taken: 18–24 months.
- Fee: INR 4,500 (e-filing, individuals/startups); INR 9,000 (others) per class.
- Protects for 10 years, renewable.

### Patent
- Protects novel inventions for 20 years.
- File with Indian Patent Office (ipindia.gov.in).
- DPIIT-recognised startups get 80% rebate on patent filing fees.
- Fast-track examination available for startups.
- Time to grant: 2–5 years typically.

### Copyright
- Automatic upon creation; registration is optional but recommended.
- Register at copyright.gov.in.
- Protects software code, creative works, designs.

### Design Registration
- Protects the visual appearance of a product under the Designs Act, 2000.
- Valid for 10 years, extendable by 5 years.

## Key Contracts and Legal Documents

### Founders Agreement
- Defines roles, responsibilities, equity split, vesting, decision-making rights.
- Should be signed before incorporating.
- Includes IP assignment clause – all IP created by founders is assigned to the company.

### Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP)
- Must be approved by board and shareholders.
- SEBI guidelines for listed companies; Companies Act for unlisted.
- Vesting typically over 4 years with 1-year cliff.
- Tax event occurs at exercise (perquisite tax) and at sale (capital gains).

### Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
- Use before sharing sensitive business information with potential partners, employees, investors.

### Shareholder Agreement (SHA)
- Signed between all shareholders; governs rights, transfer restrictions, drag-along, tag-along.

### Term Sheet
- Non-binding agreement outlining key terms of an investment round.

### Employment Agreement
- Mandatory for all full-time employees.
- Must include: designation, CTC, notice period, confidentiality, IP assignment, non-compete.

## Data Privacy and Cybersecurity

### Information Technology Act, 2000
- Section 43A: Compensation for failure to protect sensitive personal data.
- Section 72A: Punishment for disclosure of information in breach of contract.

### Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA)
- India's comprehensive data protection law (aligned with GDPR principles).
- Requires: consent for data processing, data minimisation, right to erasure.
- Designate a Data Protection Officer (DPO) if processing large-scale sensitive data.

## Annual Compliance Calendar (Pvt Ltd)

| Month        | Compliance Activity                                              |
|--------------|------------------------------------------------------------------|
| April        | TDS returns for Q4 (previous year); Advance Tax 1st instalment  |
| June         | Advance Tax 2nd instalment                                       |
| July         | Income Tax return filing (extended deadline possible)            |
| September    | Advance Tax 3rd instalment; AGM (within 6 months of FY end)     |
| October      | Board meeting (within 60 days of AGM)                            |
| December     | Advance Tax 4th instalment; ROC Annual Return filing             |
| Ongoing      | Monthly GST returns (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B), TDS deduction & payment  |
