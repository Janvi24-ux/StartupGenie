STARTUP BUDGET AND FINANCIAL PLANNING – KNOWLEDGE BASE
========================================================

## Startup Budget Categories

### Technology / Product Development
- MVP (Minimum Viable Product) development cost ranges:
  - No-code MVP (Bubble, Webflow): INR 0 – 50,000
  - Simple web app MVP: INR 1 lakh – 5 lakh
  - Complex web/mobile app MVP: INR 5 lakh – 30 lakh
  - Enterprise-grade platform: INR 30 lakh – 2 crore+
- Annual cloud infrastructure cost (AWS/Azure/GCP):
  - Early stage (< 1,000 users): INR 10,000 – 1 lakh/year
  - Growth stage (10,000–100,000 users): INR 1 lakh – 10 lakh/year
  - Scale stage (1M+ users): INR 10 lakh – 1 crore+/year
- Domain and hosting: INR 1,000 – 10,000/year
- Software licenses and tools: INR 50,000 – 5 lakh/year

### Team / Human Resources
- Average salaries in India (2024):
  - Full-stack developer (0–2 years): INR 4 lakh – 8 lakh/year
  - Full-stack developer (2–5 years): INR 8 lakh – 18 lakh/year
  - Product manager: INR 10 lakh – 25 lakh/year
  - UX/UI designer: INR 5 lakh – 15 lakh/year
  - Marketing executive: INR 3 lakh – 8 lakh/year
  - Sales executive: INR 3 lakh – 6 lakh + commission
  - Data scientist: INR 8 lakh – 20 lakh/year
  - CTO (startup): INR 20 lakh – 60 lakh/year (or equity)
- Freelancer rates:
  - Developer: INR 500 – 3,000/hour
  - Designer: INR 400 – 2,000/hour
  - Content writer: INR 1 – 5/word

### Office and Operations
- Co-working space (major metros – Mumbai, Bangalore, Delhi):
  - Hot desk: INR 5,000 – 15,000/month/person
  - Dedicated desk: INR 8,000 – 20,000/month/person
  - Private cabin: INR 20,000 – 60,000/month
- Own office (Tier 1 city):
  - Rent: INR 50 – 150 per sq ft/month
  - Security deposit: 6–12 months rent
- Utilities (electricity, internet, water): INR 5,000 – 20,000/month for small office.
- Office equipment (computers, furniture): INR 50,000 – 3 lakh initial investment.

### Marketing and Customer Acquisition
- Digital marketing:
  - Google Ads CPC: INR 10 – 200 (varies widely by industry)
  - Meta Ads CPM: INR 50 – 500
  - LinkedIn Ads CPC: INR 150 – 600
  - SEO agency: INR 15,000 – 60,000/month
  - Social media management: INR 10,000 – 40,000/month
- Offline marketing:
  - Pamphlets/flyers: INR 2,000 – 10,000 per run
  - Events/expos: INR 50,000 – 5 lakh per event
  - PR agency: INR 30,000 – 1 lakh/month
- Brand identity (logo + guidelines): INR 10,000 – 1 lakh

### Legal and Professional
- Company incorporation: INR 5,000 – 15,000 (government fees + CA)
- GST registration: INR 0 – 2,000
- Trademark registration: INR 4,500 – 9,000 per class
- Patent filing: INR 1,750 – 8,800 (startup rate after 80% rebate)
- Annual compliance (CA): INR 30,000 – 1.5 lakh/year
- Legal consultation: INR 3,000 – 10,000/hour (lawyer)

## Bootstrapping Budget Estimates by Stage

### Idea Stage (INR 0 – 5 lakh)
- Laptop and basic tools.
- Domain, hosting.
- Market research tools.
- Initial prototype (no-code or self-built).

### Pre-seed/MVP Stage (INR 5 lakh – 30 lakh)
- MVP development.
- Small team (1–3 people) or freelancers.
- Minimal marketing for early user feedback.
- Company incorporation and basic legal.

### Seed Stage (INR 30 lakh – 2 crore)
- Full team hiring (5–15 people).
- Product scaling post-MVP.
- Initial paid marketing.
- Office space.
- Working capital.

### Series A (INR 2 crore – 20 crore)
- Large team (15–50 people).
- Multi-channel marketing.
- Enterprise infrastructure.
- Expansion to new geographies.

## Financial Metrics Benchmarks

### SaaS Startups
- Magic Number (Sales efficiency): Target > 0.75.
- Rule of 40: Revenue growth % + EBITDA margin % > 40%.
- Gross Revenue Retention (GRR): > 90% best-in-class.
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR): > 110% (expansion revenue).
- ARR per FTE: INR 25 lakh+ for early stage.

### E-Commerce / D2C
- Gross Margin: 40–60%.
- Repeat Purchase Rate: > 30% within 90 days.
- Return Rate: < 15%.
- CAC:LTV ratio: 1:3 or better.

### Marketplace
- Take Rate: 10–25%.
- GMV growth: > 100% YoY in early stages.
- Liquidity: Fill rate (% of demand fulfilled by supply) > 70%.

## 3-Year Financial Projection Template

### Year 1
- Focus: MVP launch, first 100 customers, product-market fit validation.
- Typical Revenue: INR 5 lakh – 50 lakh (depends on model).
- Burn Rate: INR 3 lakh – 15 lakh/month.
- Key Milestone: First paying customers, break-even on unit economics.

### Year 2
- Focus: Growth, marketing, team expansion.
- Typical Revenue: 5–10x Year 1.
- Burn Rate: INR 10 lakh – 50 lakh/month.
- Key Milestone: Series A funding, 1,000+ customers.

### Year 3
- Focus: Market expansion, operational efficiency, profitability path.
- Typical Revenue: 3–5x Year 2.
- Key Milestone: Path to EBITDA break-even.
